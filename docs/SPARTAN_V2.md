# Spartan v2 — adversarial evolutionary qualification

## Purpose
Spartan v2 selects deployable economic robustness rather than nominal historical yield.
It supplements, never replaces, chronological train/validation/untouched-holdout testing.

## Qualification sequence
1. Historical discovery. Evolution may see training data only.
2. Chronological validation. Candidates must retain positive net expectancy.
3. Untouched holdout. Holdout never shapes breeding or finalist selection.
4. Execution torture. Monte Carlo friction, slippage and latency perturbation.
5. Path survival. Evaluate drawdown and CVaR-like lower-tail outcomes.
6. Opportunity efficiency. Credit correct rejection and penalise missed winners/bad entries.
7. Behavioural diversity. Distinct parameters do not count if opportunity sets overlap.
8. Prospective paper. Only unseen future observations can earn live-canary eligibility.

## Execution torture
Each candidate is replayed across many perturbed execution paths. Normal friction dominates,
but random 1.5x, 2x and 3x friction shocks occur alongside adverse latency/slippage drag.
A candidate must remain positive at the stressed mean, 5th percentile and lower-tail CVaR.

## Risk policy
A 15% drawdown is not an automatic historical death sentence from one tiny sample.
Instead, at least 95% of stressed paths must remain within the survival envelope.
Catastrophic behaviour remains a hard rejection in the existing nursery gates.

## Colony topology
Queens remain strategy hypotheses rather than fixed role labels. Ants specialise within Queens;
the Swarm Queen eventually allocates capital only among independently demonstrated edges.
Directional inversion/mirror tests are diagnostics: failure is interpreted against strategy intent,
not used as a universal rejection rule.

## Diversity
Use behavioural Jaccard distance over actual opportunity/event IDs. Five genomes taking the same
eight trades are one economic organism for portfolio-diversification purposes, regardless of
parameter distance. Preserve fresh random genomes in discovery populations to prevent stagnation.

## Economic objective
Optimise deployable pounds after realistic execution, not headline percentage return. Fitness
should increasingly include capacity/liquidity estimates as those measurements become reliable.
No historical or adversarial score can authorise live capital; prospective evidence remains the gate.

## 2026-09-29 baseline
Wallet Convergence: shelved after 400,000 Spartan attempts produced zero graduates.
Exhaustion: five historical Spartans found; now prospective-paper only.
Momentum: original and Early Acceleration formulations failed chronological robustness; shelved.
Order Flow: original formulation failed as evidence bank grew. Breakout Failure replacement
completed 50,000 genomes on 1,857 rows / 162 mints with zero eligible candidates and zero finalists.
This is retained as a clean pre-v2 falsification baseline.

## 2 October 2026 — external examiner, Hall of Fame and Alumni

Spartan remains an external examiner. Its exact thresholds and sealed answers are not returned to the Breeding Queen or mutation-credit system. Queen v8 may learn which mutation operators improve train+validation descendants, but Spartan evidence cannot alter those operator weights.

Failed finalists are no longer treated as disposable information. After each completed exam, up to the top three behaviourally distinct performers are copied into a permanent Hall of Fame with exam provenance and metrics. This archive does not convert failure into a pass.

Selected Hall-of-Fame/finalist genomes may seed a quarantined Spartan Alumni paper pool. Alumni ancestry is explicitly exam-contaminated: old exam evidence may nominate genetics, but descendants start with zero prospective proof and must earn fresh forward evidence before any future untouched examination. Cosmetic/genetic duplicates do not receive extra archival slots.


## Exact hold-time exploration

Spartan Alumni are not restricted to the evaluator's canonical horizon grid. Active alumni may explore exact hold durations from 3 to 240 minutes. When an eligible alumni entry uses a noncanonical hold, the system registers that candidate/horizon prospectively in `research_outcome_requests`; the forward evaluator measures it when that exact horizon matures. Requests made after the target horizon has already passed are rejected rather than manufacturing retrospective evidence.

A small `hold_probe` lane deliberately varies only `hold_minutes` around a parent while holding entry predicates constant. This separates exit-timing discovery from broader genome mutation. Alumni descendants still begin with zero inherited prospective proof, and ordinary parent-improvement / tail / concentration rules continue to apply once sufficient paired evidence exists.
