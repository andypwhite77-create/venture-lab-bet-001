## Queen-directed consolidation

Their code and historical evidence are retained as negative knowledge; they do not consume active evolutionary budget. Mean Reversion remains research-only rather than promoted. The Queen's general pattern-recognition programme is now the primary discovery budget. She is given the rules of the economic game—positive after-cost expectancy, breadth, survival, distributed contribution and chronological persistence—but not Spartan's hidden pass thresholds. Search capacity is increased to six waves of 50,000 genomes with 300-parent retention and 10% fresh random blood. Holdout remains sealed during breeding; Spartan remains an unseen examiner.

## Reversal tournament repair
Audit found the original frozen Reversal baseline remained prospectively positive while most evolved descendants degraded it. The tournament was also stuck at stage 0 because asynchronous replacement/backfill kept changing the evidence-bearing cohort and the first-stage evidence threshold was mismatched to the current observation rate.

Reversal is now treated as a protected parent/control experiment. Asynchronous challenger displacement and mid-stage backfill are disabled for Reversal; descendants must beat the frozen parent rather than replace or obscure it. Stage evidence gates remain prospective but are staged at 12/20/30/40 independent observations before the final five face 50 new observations. The existing run immediately advanced 100 -> 60, culling 39 descendants while preserving the immortal baseline. No change grants live-money authority.
